Milestone 10 adds placeholder-only integration settings.

Supplier:

- `SUPPLIER_MODE=mock`
- `SUPPLIER_PRIORITY=MOCK,BCI,ABHIBUS,REDBUS,TBO,CUSTOM`
- `SUPPLIER_CONNECTION_TIMEOUT_MS=1500`
- `SUPPLIER_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_MS=3000`
- `SUPPLIER_RETRY_COUNT=1`
- `SUPPLIER_RETRY_DELAY_MS=150`
- `SUPPLIER_CIRCUIT_BREAKER_THRESHOLD=3`
- `SUPPLIER_CIRCUIT_BREAKER_COOLDOWN_MS=30000`
- `BCI_API_URL=`
- `BCI_API_KEY=`
- `REDBUS_API_URL=`
- `REDBUS_API_KEY=`
- `ABHIBUS_API_URL=`
- `ABHIBUS_API_KEY=`
